A Randomized Controlled Trial of Novel Treatment Intervention

Authors: Smith J, Johnson A, Williams B
Journal: Journal of Medical Science, 2024
DOI: 10.1234/jms.2024.12345
Download: 📄 View PDF ⬇️ Download PDF

Abstract

Background: This is a test abstract for the Q-SCI extension PDF analysis feature. The study was designed as a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial to evaluate the efficacy of a novel intervention.

Methods: We enrolled 500 participants aged 18-65 years and randomly assigned them to receive either the active treatment or placebo. The primary outcome was measured at 12 weeks using validated assessment tools. Sample size calculation was based on 80% power to detect a clinically significant difference.

Results: The intervention group showed significant improvement compared to placebo (p < 0.001). Safety analysis revealed no serious adverse events. The study followed CONSORT guidelines for reporting.

Conclusions: This randomized controlled trial demonstrates the efficacy and safety of the novel intervention. The findings support its use in clinical practice.

Introduction

Scientific research requires rigorous methodology to ensure reliable results. This study employed a randomized controlled trial design, considered the gold standard for evaluating interventions. The protocol was registered prospectively and followed international guidelines.

Study Design

This was a parallel-group, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial. Participants were stratified by age and gender. Randomization was performed using a computer-generated sequence with concealed allocation. Both participants and investigators were blinded to treatment assignment throughout the study period.

Statistical Analysis

Sample size calculation determined that 500 participants would provide 80% power to detect a clinically meaningful difference. Analysis followed the intention-to-treat principle. Missing data were handled using multiple imputation. All statistical tests were two-tailed with significance set at p < 0.05.
