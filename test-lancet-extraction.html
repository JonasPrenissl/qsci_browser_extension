Effectiveness of Vaccination Programs in Rural Communities

Introduction: Vaccination programs are critical for public health, yet rural communities often face unique challenges in accessing these services.

Methods: We conducted a comprehensive evaluation of vaccination programs across 50 rural communities, analyzing coverage rates and barriers to access.

Results: Overall vaccination coverage was 78%, with significant variation between communities. Key barriers included transportation access and health literacy.

Conclusion: Targeted interventions addressing specific barriers can significantly improve vaccination coverage in rural settings.
